Built for serious investors

Every screen is engineered to help you understand your money faster — across U.S., European, and Asian markets.

🌍

Global markets

Browse a curated universe of leading stocks from NYSE, NASDAQ, London, Tokyo, Hong Kong, and Frankfurt. Filter by sector and scan top movers.

📈

Detailed charts

Professional K-line candlesticks with six time periods, interactive crosshair, and mark-to-market indicators. Sparklines on every holding.

💼

Portfolio that works

Log buys and sells, attach receipts, set fees. We auto-compute true average cost, unrealized P/L, realized gains, sector allocation, and a 60-day curve.

🛠

Smart tools

Convert between ten major currencies, run a compound growth calculator with monthly contributions, and export your data as JSON, CSV, or summary.

💡

Insights

Read market news grouped by sentiment, study sector heatmaps with live performance, and watch global indices on one screen.

🔒

Privacy first

No account. No login. No tracking. All your trades, notes, and photos stay on your device. You own your data and can export or reset anytime.

Designed for iPhone. Built dark, fast, and focused.

iOS 16 and above. Native SwiftUI. Engineered to feel right at home on your device.

Your money, your device

Every trade, receipt, note, and watchlist entry is stored locally on your iPhone. Nothing leaves the device unless you export it yourself.

Multi-currency, by design

Hold U.S. dollars, euros, yen, pounds, and more. ENCENE converts holdings into your base currency using built-in reference rates.

Mock market data

For demonstration, ENCENE simulates prices, charts, and news. It is a tracking and journaling tool — not a brokerage or advisor.

No advice, no surprises

ENCENE does not provide investment recommendations. A clear risk disclaimer is presented the first time you use the app.

Free download. No signup. Just open the app and start tracking.
