Privacy Policy

ENCENE respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect — which, in short, is none.

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Summary

ENCENE is a personal portfolio tracking and journaling app. We do not require an account, we do not collect personal information, and we do not track you across other apps or websites. All the data you create — trades, receipts, notes, profile information, watchlist — is stored locally on your device.

Information We Collect

Because ENCENE is designed to run entirely on your device, the only data the app processes is the data you enter yourself:

  • Profile information — name and initials you choose to display.
  • Trades and holdings — buy/sell entries, prices, fees, and dates you log.
  • Receipts and notes — photos and text you attach to trades.
  • Watchlist and preferences — tickers you follow, base currency, appearance settings.

This data lives only on your device. It is not uploaded to our servers, because we do not operate servers that store your content.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No account or login information (we have no sign-up flow).
  • No contact list, location, advertising ID, or device identifiers linked to you.
  • No analytics or behavioral tracking inside the app.
  • No third-party tracking SDKs that follow you across other apps or websites.

Market Data and News

For demonstration, ENCENE ships with simulated market prices, charts, indices, and news content. These are mock datasets bundled with the app and do not represent real-time market data. They are not transmitted from or to any third party at runtime.

Permissions

ENCENE may request the following iOS permissions, only at the moment they are needed:

  • Photo Library — to attach a receipt photo to a trade. Photos you pick remain in your library; we never read your full library.
  • Notifications (optional) — to send you trade-related reminders if you opt in.

You can revoke any permission at any time in iOS Settings.

Children's Privacy

ENCENE is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, this policy applies uniformly.

Your Rights and Controls

You stay in control of your data at all times:

  • Export — use the in-app export feature to produce a JSON, CSV, or summary file at any time.
  • Reset — the Settings screen lets you wipe all local data and reload the sample dataset.
  • Delete the app — removing ENCENE from your device permanently deletes all local data.

International Users

Because ENCENE does not transfer your personal data to our servers or to any third party, regional data-transfer regulations (such as the GDPR in the EEA/UK or similar laws elsewhere) do not require us to perform cross-border transfers of your content.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the app or to legal requirements. The "Last updated" date at the top will always reflect when the policy was last revised. Material changes will be highlighted in-app where appropriate.
