Common topics

Getting started

Open ENCENE, accept the risk disclaimer, and the sample portfolio loads so you can explore every feature immediately. No sign-up required.

Logging trades

Use the Trade tab to add buys and sells. Set the date, price, quantity, fees, currency, and an optional receipt photo or note.

Multi-currency

Pick your base currency in Settings. Holdings in other currencies convert using ENCENE's built-in reference rates.

Exports

Settings → Export Data produces JSON, CSV, or a formatted summary you can share or back up.

Charts and data

Prices, charts, and news in the app are simulated for demonstration. They are not real-time market data.

Reset and delete

Settings → Reset All Data clears local content and reloads the sample dataset. Deleting the app removes everything.

Frequently asked questions

Does ENCENE connect to my brokerage?

No. ENCENE is a manual tracking and journaling app. You enter your trades yourself; we do not sync with brokerages.

Are the prices real?

No. ENCENE ships with simulated market data for demonstration. Do not use the app for actual trading decisions.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone only. ENCENE does not upload your trades, receipts, or notes to any server.

Can I get my data out?

Yes. Use the in-app Export feature any time to produce a JSON, CSV, or summary file.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone running iOS 16 and above.
